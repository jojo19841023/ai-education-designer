# Mid-Video Knowledge Board Prompt Pattern

Use this pattern for frames that need to carry spoken explanation in long-form video.

## Pattern

- 16:9 widescreen
- light grey grid paper
- black pencil sketch
- clear subtitle-safe area at bottom
- one medium-weight scholarly Chinese title at top
- very few short handwritten labels
- restrained but visible accent colors
- one dominant structural idea per frame

## English template

```text
A hand-drawn educational knowledge-board illustration on light grey grid paper, 16:9 widescreen. Black pencil sketch with visible construction lines and restrained but readable accent colors. Designed for the middle section of a long-form Chinese knowledge video. One medium-weight modern scholarly Chinese title at the top. Minimal handwritten Chinese labels only. Spacious composition, clean subtitle-safe area at the bottom, serious and elegant knowledge-video style.
```

## Avoid

- full explanatory sentences inside the image
- crowded infographic logic
- thumbnail-level oversized titles for mid-video frames
- excessive decorative icons
